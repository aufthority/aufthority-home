Metabolic & heart health · Risk calculator

CerminDiri

A metabolic and heart-risk self-check calibrated for Malaysians, using Asia-Pacific standards that apply across ASEAN rather than Western norms.

  • ProblemMost online risk calculators use Western cut-offs and cohorts. Asian populations carry higher cardiometabolic risk at a lower BMI and waist size, so those tools can under-call risk.
  • MethodThree established scores, each chosen for its evidence in Asian or Malaysian populations: FINDRISC with Asian waist thresholds for diabetes, INTERHEART (validated in Malaysian adults) for heart-attack risk, and Framingham (validated in Malaysia) for 10-year cardiovascular risk.
  • LocalisedWHO Asia-Pacific BMI and waist cut-offs, and a chart that places each user against Malaysian population data from NHMS 2019 and 2023, by sex and age.
  • LimitsEach score's origin and known biases are stated on the page, so users know where an estimate may over- or under-call.
  • ReachMy most-used tool. A viral Threads spike didn't become lasting search traffic; I traced it to an indexing problem, which is next to fix.

Behaviour change · PWA · Beta

NeuroHabit

A habit tracker built on neuroscience: habits grouped by four neurochemical pathways (dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin and endorphin) that feed one Hero Score.

  • ProblemMost habit apps reward streaks without explaining the behaviour behind them, or how habits depend on each other.
  • MethodA literature-review matrix for every habit: the mechanism linking it to the dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin or endorphin pathway, and the strength of the evidence behind it, graded strong or moderate. Extrapolated links, such as cold exposure and endorphins, are flagged as extrapolated rather than presented as fact.
  • EngineThe matrix sets the scoring engine. A foundational habit unlocks each category, secondary habits count double bonus habits, and a habit set locks for 28 days, following Lally (2010) and Keller (2021) rather than the popular 21-day claim.
  • BuiltAn offline-first PWA with a coach feed, multi-session cyclers and shareable score images, written in compassionate, non-judgemental language.
  • LearningDownloads came; keeping users is the open problem. The next step is learning from the returning core before building further.

Interactive chemistry labs

Four standalone labs that show what particles are doing inside a flask, and why a trend runs the way it does. Each one is layered, so the same page serves a first-year student and final-exam revision.

Core secondary school Go deeper STPM / Matriculation Into the maths degree
Acid–Base Lab Strong and weak acids, equilibrium, titration curves, and equivalence point versus end point. Atomic Orbital Lab s, p and d orbitals in 3D, with clouds, surfaces, nodes and cutaways you can rotate. Titration & Gravimetry Lab Acid–base, precipitation, redox and EDTA titrations, plus gravimetry. Students add the drops themselves. Periodic Trends Lab Effective nuclear charge, size, ionisation energy and electronegativity, recoloured across a live periodic table.

No sign-in needed. English, with British spelling and standard chemical notation.
