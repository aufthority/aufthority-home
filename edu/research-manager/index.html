Sixty references. A new style. How long?

Your lecturer, or the journal, asks you to change every reference from APA to Vancouver. Typed by hand, that is hours of work, and mistakes slip in. With a reference manager, it takes seconds.

This explainer shows what reference managers are, why they matter, and how to use one step by step, with Zotero as the worked example.

Definition

What is a reference manager?

A reference manager is software that stores the details of every source you read (authors, title, journal, year, DOI) together with its PDF, in one personal library.

When you write, it inserts citations into your text and builds the reference list for you, in whatever style you choose.

Think of your phone's contacts. You save a number once. After that, you never need to remember or retype it to make a call.

Key concept

Four jobs, always in this order.

1. Collectcapture the source
2. Organisefile it by project
3. Readannotate the PDF
4. Citeinsert and format
  1. 1Collect: capture a source's details straight from a journal page, a database or PubMed.
  2. 2Organise: group sources by project, topic or chapter.
  3. 3Read and annotate: highlight and take notes on the PDF itself.
  4. 4Cite: insert citations as you write and generate the reference list.

Every major reference manager does these four jobs. What differs is the cost, the owner and the details of how each works.

Check your understanding

Two quick questions.

QUESTION 1 OF 2
What does a reference manager store?
It stores the structured details of each source (and usually the PDF). Because the details are structured, it can format them in any style. A list saved only as text cannot be reformatted.
QUESTION 2 OF 2
You are typing your assignment in Word. Which of the four jobs happens there?
Citing happens in your word processor, through the reference manager's plugin. Collecting and organising happen earlier, in the library itself.

Know your tools.

QUESTION 1 OF 2
A senior tells you to download "Mendeley Desktop". What should you know?
Mendeley still exists, but the old Desktop app was replaced. Advice from a few years ago may point to software you can no longer install.
QUESTION 2 OF 2
Which tool is free and open source?
Zotero. Mendeley is free but owned by a commercial publisher, Elsevier, and is not open source. EndNote is paid software from Clarivate.
Step 1 · Install

Three things to install.

  1. 1Zotero desktop: download it from zotero.org/download. This is your library.
  2. 2Zotero Connector: an extension for your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari). This is your capture button.
  3. 3A free Zotero account: register at zotero.org, then sign in from Zotero under Settings → Sync. This backs up your library and keeps your devices in step.
Writing plugins. The Word and LibreOffice plugins are installed with Zotero desktop, so close Word while installing. For Google Docs, the Zotero menu appears once the Connector is installed. (Zotero Documentation)
Step 2 · Collect

Three ways to add a source.

Open each one before moving on.

Open the article on PubMed or the journal's website and click the Zotero Connector icon in your browser. The details are saved at once, along with the PDF when you have access to it.

In Zotero, click the magic wand (Add Item by Identifier) and paste a DOI, PMID or ISBN. Zotero looks up the details for you.

Drag the PDF into Zotero. It will try to retrieve the paper's details automatically.

(Zotero Documentation)

Step 3 · Organise

Collections work like playlists.

Collections group items by project, assignment or chapter. One item can sit in several collections at once without being duplicated, just as one song can be in many playlists. Removing an item from a collection does not delete it from your library.

Tags are free labels that cut across collections, such as methods or to-read. You can also search by author, title, tag or the full text of your PDFs.

(Zotero Documentation)

Step 4 · Read

Read and annotate inside Zotero.

Double-click a PDF to open it in Zotero's built-in reader. Highlight key sentences and add notes. Use colours with a meaning, for example yellow for main findings and red for limitations.

Annotations are stored with the item in your library. You can turn them into a note, and quotes in that note keep a link back to their source.

Zotero 10 adds Reading Mode: a clean, reflowed view without two-column layouts, with adjustable font size. You can still highlight and add notes in it. (Zotero, 2026)

A habit that pays off. For each paper, write one or two sentences in your own words: what it found, and why it matters to you. When you write your literature review, half the work is done.
Step 5 · Cite

Cite as you write.

The Zotero tab in Microsoft Word
Add/Edit CitationAdd/Edit BibliographyDocument PreferencesRefresh
  1. 1Click Document Preferences and choose a style, such as APA or Vancouver.
  2. 2Put the cursor where the citation goes, click Add/Edit Citation, type an author's name or a word from the title, and press Enter.
  3. 3At the end of the document, click Add/Edit Bibliography. The list is built and kept up to date for you.
  4. 4To add a page number, type it after the item before pressing Enter, for example p.34.

(Zotero Documentation)

Changing the style in Document Preferences reformats every citation and the whole reference list at once. Try all three styles.

Journals and lecturers often use slightly different versions of a style. Install the exact one you need from the Zotero Style Repository.

Step 5 · Cite

Fix the library, not the document.

Do not edit citations or the reference list by hand in Word. An edited citation stops updating automatically, and manual changes to the bibliography are overwritten the next time Zotero refreshes. Correct the item in Zotero, then click Refresh. (Zotero Documentation)

In Zotero, open Settings → Cite and click Get additional styles. This opens the Zotero Style Repository. Search for the journal or publisher, then click to install the style.

The workflow is the same. In Google Docs, use the Zotero menu in the top menu bar. In LibreOffice, use the Zotero toolbar. Zotero can also move a cited document between Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice.

Check your understanding

Three questions on Zotero.

QUESTION 1 OF 3
You add a paper to a second collection. What happens to it?
Collections work like playlists: one item, many collections, no duplicate. Moving rather than adding needs a held modifier key while dragging.
QUESTION 2 OF 3
Where do you change the citation style of a Word document?
Document Preferences, in the Zotero tab. Word's own References tab is a separate system that does not use your Zotero library.
QUESTION 3 OF 3
An author's name is misspelt in your reference list. What is the right fix?
Fix the source of the error, the item in your library, then Refresh. Typing over it in Word is overwritten at the next refresh, and the error stays in your library for the next assignment.
Safety net

A warning before you cite a retracted paper.

Zotero checks your library against the Retraction Watch database. Retracted items are flagged in your items list, with details of the retraction.

If you try to cite a retracted item in Word, Zotero warns you first. If a paper is retracted after you cited it, the warning appears when the document is refreshed.

The catch: it only works for items with a DOI or PMID. Another reason to keep your item details complete.

(Zotero Blog)

Storage

What's free, and what isn't.

Unlimited
Item details, notes, tags and collections sync free.
300 MB
Free cloud storage for files such as PDFs. Paid plans: 2 GB, 6 GB or unlimited.

Check the size of a few of your own PDFs for a realistic average. If storage fills up, items and PDFs stay on your computer; only syncing files to the cloud is limited. Files in a group library count against the group owner's storage. (Zotero; Bates College Library)

The limit

Right format, wrong claim.

A meta-analysis of 46 studies, covering about 32,000 quotations in the medical literature, found that 16.9% were inaccurate: the cited source did not support what the author wrote. About half were major errors (8.0%), and the rate has not improved over time. (Baethge & Jergas, 2025)

Zotero makes sure your references are formatted correctly. It cannot check that a paper actually says what you claim it says.

The tool manages references. Reading, understanding and citing honestly are still your job.
Check your understanding

Two questions on the limits.

QUESTION 1 OF 2
The 16.9% figure describes:
These are errors of content, not formatting. A reference can be perfectly formatted and still point to a paper that does not say what the author claims.
QUESTION 2 OF 2
Your project group shares a Zotero group library and attaches many PDFs. Whose storage do the files use?
Group file storage draws from the owner's account, so a busy group can fill the owner's free 300 MB quickly.
Check your understanding

Five questions, no marks recorded.

QUESTION 1 OF 5
You must switch a 60-reference essay from APA to Vancouver. The best way is to:
Document Preferences reformats every citation and the reference list at once. Retyping or copying one by one is slow and reintroduces errors.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
A citation shows "n.d." instead of a year. The most likely cause is:
"n.d." means no date: the Date field was not captured. Fill it in in Zotero and Refresh. A missing PDF does not affect the citation.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Your free 300 MB of storage is full. What happens?
Your library keeps working and nothing is deleted. Only syncing files to the cloud is limited. Item details keep syncing, because they do not count against storage.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Zotero's retraction alert can only check an item that has:
The check matches identifiers. Without a DOI or PMID, a retracted paper may not be flagged, even if its PDF is attached.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
Every reference in your essay is perfectly formatted by Zotero. Does that mean your citations are accurate?
Formatting and accuracy are different things. Only reading the source tells you whether it supports what you wrote.
Take home

Save once, cite anywhere.

1
A reference manager is a personal library and a citation engine. The four jobs are collect, organise, read and cite.
2
Zotero is free and open source, and works with Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice. The skills transfer to Mendeley and EndNote.
3
Check every item as you capture it, and fix errors in the library, never by hand in the document.
4
Use the safety nets (retraction alerts, complete identifiers), but remember: the tool checks format, not content. Read your sources.
