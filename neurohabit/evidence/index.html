NeuroHabit · Evidence matrix

How every habit earns its weight.

NeuroHabit groups daily habits by the brain pathway they most plausibly act on: dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin or endorphin. Before a habit enters the app, it goes through this matrix: what the mechanism is, how strong the evidence is, and which literature it rests on.

The grades then set each habit's tier, and the tier sets its weight in the scoring engine. Weak evidence cannot earn a heavy weight.

How to read the matrix

Each habit is graded twice, because a plausible mechanism and a proven benefit are different claims.

Mechanism evidence

How well established the link is between the habit and its named pathway, from human, animal or imaging studies.

Outcome evidence

How well established the effect is on mood, wellbeing or mental-health outcomes in people.

Grades

Strong
Meta-analyses or multiple randomised trials
Moderate
Some trials, or consistent observational and mechanistic work
Limited
Plausible, but indirect, small or animal-only evidence

From grade to score

Three tiers carry different weight. Foundational habits unlock a category's full score, secondary habits score at weight 2, and bonus habits at weight 1. Each category's foundational habits:

Dopamine

Drive & reward

Deep Work / Main Goal · Meaningful Progress · Morning Sunlight (10–15 min)

Serotonin

Stability & mood

Sleep · Solat / Daily Prayer / Mindfulness · Rhythmic Movement (20+ min)

Oxytocin

Connection & trust

Active Socialising (20+ min) · Physical Touch / Oxytocin Anchor · Sincere Appreciation (Expressed)

Endorphin

Resilience & movement

Exercise (Dedicated Session) · Active Day (Incidental Steps)

Rhythmic Movement (20+ min)Serotonin Foundational Moderate Moderate Jacobs & Azmitia 1992, Physiol Rev Rhythmic motor activity increases serotonergic neuron firing in animal work; walking interventions show moderate mood benefits in people.
5-a-Day (Veg & Fruit)Serotonin Secondary Moderate Moderate Jacka et al. 2017, BMC Med; Firth et al. 2019, Psychosom Med Dietary improvement reduces depressive symptoms in trials. Note: most serotonin is made in the gut but does not cross into the brain; the link runs through the microbiome and inflammation.
Nature Walk (20+ min)Serotonin Secondary Moderate Moderate Bratman et al. 2015, PNAS A nature walk reduced rumination and subgenual prefrontal activity versus an urban walk. Small trial; consistent with wider nature-exposure literature.
Protein-Rich MealsSerotonin Secondary Moderate Limited Tryptophan precursor literature Tryptophan is the serotonin precursor, but protein-rich meals also raise competing amino acids, so brain entry depends on the whole meal. Mood outcomes are limited.
Gratitude / MuhasabahSerotonin Bonus Limited Moderate Davis et al. 2016, J Couns Psychol Gratitude interventions show small but consistent wellbeing effects. The serotonin mechanism is inferred.
Breathwork / DhikrSerotonin Bonus Moderate Moderate Zaccaro et al. 2018, Front Hum Neurosci; Goyal et al. 2014 Slow breathing shifts autonomic balance toward parasympathetic activity. Effects on anxiety are modest but real.

Restorative Hobby (20+ min)Serotonin Secondary Limited Moderate Soga et al. 2017, Prev Med Rep (gardening meta-analysis) Gardening and similar hobbies show positive wellbeing effects. Soil-bacteria serotonin findings are from animal studies only.

Community Help / Act of KindnessOxytocin Bonus Moderate Moderate Moll et al. 2006, PNAS; Curry et al. 2018, J Exp Soc Psychol Giving activates reward circuitry, and kindness interventions show small-to-medium wellbeing effects.

Exercise (Dedicated Session)Endorphin Foundational Strong Strong Singh et al. 2023, Br J Sports Med (97 reviews, n=128,119); Boecker et al. 2008, Cereb Cortex Exercise reduces depression and anxiety with medium effects; PET shows opioid release in running. Tiered by intensity.

Yoga / Mobility / Stretching (10+ min)Endorphin Bonus Moderate Moderate Cramer et al. 2013, Depress Anxiety Yoga shows moderate short-term benefits for depressive symptoms.

What the review changed

The matrix is not decoration: grading the evidence changed the engine.

PromotedMorning SunlightRaised to foundational, so Dopamine no longer depends on a single habit that some users cannot do daily.
ReweightedTask List CompletionKept secondary but reduced: real but small reward signals did not justify a heavy weight.
MovedSocial LaughterFrom Endorphin to Oxytocin. Its function is bonding, even though PET shows opioid release.
SoftenedCold exposure and touch claimsCold water is credited mainly with noradrenaline, with the endorphin link marked as extrapolated; "20-second hug" became "sustained contact".
RebalancedSerotonin weightsFoundational weights reduced so one category could not dominate the daily score.
RemovedVirtual check-insWeak evidence relative to in-person contact, and overlapping with Active Socialising.

What this is, and isn't. NeuroHabit is a habit-building and psychoeducation tool, not a treatment for any condition. Mapping a habit to one neurotransmitter is a simplification: every habit here acts on several systems at once.

The grades reflect the literature as reviewed in October 2026 and will change as evidence does.
